About

Your page loads Block's Pay Kit (sandbox.kit.cash.app/v1/pay.js). The SDK initializes it, renders the Cash App Pay button and reports the shopper's decision.

Flow steps

setup() → beginCheckout({ amount }) → shopper approves → cashAppApproved → cashAppTokenGenerated → purchase on your server.

Testing notes

On mobile the shopper returns to this page (the redirectURL), and the approval arrives right after setup(). Pay Kit needs an https redirectURL for that, so test the redirect on Heroku or through a tunnel.
